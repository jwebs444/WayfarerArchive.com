# Wayfarer's Archive search contract

- Discovery posture: broadly indexable Traditional project storefront with five
  distinct public route jobs and no keyword, location, product, or edition
  variants.
- Route/query ownership: `/` owns the offline-knowledge project overview;
  `/build` owns the planned free offline-Wikipedia-drive builder and its current
  validation gate; `/build-beyond` owns personal offline-library extension and
  source/rights boundaries; `/founding-batch` owns interest in a possible
  prepared field edition; and `/creator` owns the public Mr. Crowmeister
  relationship.
- Readiness locks: the builder remains in validation; the physical pilot has not
  established price, capacity, policies, or a credible shipping window; no
  payment, order, reservation, shipment date, or public installer is offered.
- Rights locks: the public edition is planned around redistributable material;
  third-party personal-library recommendations are source links, not included or
  redistributed files.
- Crawl/index contract: all five public routes are indexable and self-canonical;
  `robots.txt` allows public crawling and advertises a sitemap containing only
  those five routes. Unknown routes are `noindex`.
- Canonical host: metadata, social URLs, and sitemap entries resolve against
  `https://wayfarerarchive.com`, including preview and Worker requests.
- Structured data: the homepage identifies the `WebSite`, public `WebPage`,
  Wayfarer's Archive `CreativeWork`, and its public Mr. Crowmeister creator
  relationship without inventing commerce availability, reviews, ratings,
  release dates, prices, credentials, or private identity.
- Social previews remain a separate surface using the existing `public/og.png`
  card.
